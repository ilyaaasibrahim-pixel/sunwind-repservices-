Why "SunWind"

Sun and wind are unpredictable, aren't they? So is this business.

Demand shifts with the season. Source markets rise and fall without warning. A single trend, review or booking pattern can change the picture overnight. In hospitality and tourism, unpredictability isn't the exception — it's the environment you operate in every day.

SunWind Representations exists to help you manage that unpredictability — the way sailors have always read the sun and the wind to find their way. Through hands-on sales & marketing, PR & branding, and full operational takeovers run by our own team on a profit-sharing basis, we help hotels, guesthouses and resorts regain stronger market share and networking in key markets.
